## Two Different Businesses, Same Budget Tracking Gap

Zola and WeddingWire have different owners, different business models, and different product focuses. What they have in common is that neither built a real budget tracking tool for couples.

This is not an accident. It reflects each platform's revenue model.

Zola makes money when couples use its registry. The more couples spend on registry items purchased through Zola's platform or partner retailers, the more Zola earns. A budget tool that helps couples track every dollar and spend more deliberately would reduce registry conversion. Building a good budget ledger costs Zola money.

WeddingWire makes money when vendors pay for placement in front of couples. A budget tool that makes couples more price-sensitive and more deliberate about vendor selection would reduce the commercial value of WeddingWire's vendor directory. Building a good budget ledger costs WeddingWire money.

Both companies made reasonable business decisions. Neither decision resulted in a useful budget tracking tool for couples.

## What WeddingWire Actually Has

WeddingWire's budget section starts with your total budget and suggests category allocations based on typical wedding spending ratios. You can adjust categories, rename them, and set custom budget amounts.

You can add line items within categories and track planned spending against your category budget. The interface shows how much you have allocated versus your total.

What you cannot do: enter that a specific vendor quoted you $4,800, that you paid $1,500 as a deposit on March 10th, that your next payment of $1,650 is due June 1st, and that you have $1,650 remaining on that contract. The tool is a planning template, not a financial ledger.

## What Zola Has

Nothing in the budget area.

## The Spreadsheet Pattern

Couples who use either platform for planning almost universally maintain a separate budget tracking system - usually a Google Sheet or Excel file. This is not because they are unusually organized. It is because the platforms do not provide the tracking they need, and the financial stakes are high enough that they cannot operate without it.

Managing $25,000 to $50,000 across 10-15 vendors over 12-18 months, with deposits, installments, and final payments at different dates, requires a real tracking system. Neither Zola nor WeddingWire provides one.
