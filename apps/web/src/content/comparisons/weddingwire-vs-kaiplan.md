## The Same Company, Different Branding

Most couples don't know that WeddingWire and The Knot are the same company. Both are operated by The Knot Worldwide, a division of Dotdash Meredith. They share vendor data, back-end infrastructure, and — critically — the same advertising revenue model.

That matters because it means the "competition" between WeddingWire and The Knot is largely cosmetic. The structural conflicts that come with a vendor advertising model apply to both. Choosing between them is choosing a UI preference, not a meaningfully different product.

## What WeddingWire Offers

WeddingWire's vendor directory covers most of the same ground as The Knot, because they're pulling from the same data source. Real vendor reviews from real couples, broad geographic coverage, and enough category depth to find vendors in most major markets.

The planning checklist is standard — calendar-ordered milestones for a typical wedding. The wedding website builder is functional. Guest list and RSVP tools cover the basics. The budget tool estimates how to allocate your total budget across categories.

For couples who want a free starting point, WeddingWire checks most boxes.

## What WeddingWire Doesn't Tell You

The vendor rankings are advertising. When WeddingWire surfaces "featured" or "top-rated" vendors in a search, the position reflects advertising spend alongside organic factors. A vendor who paid for WiredQuotes (WeddingWire's lead product) gets more visibility. That's the revenue model.

The FTC complaints are worth knowing about. Over 200 formal filings since 2018 alleged fake leads sold to vendors, review manipulation, and deceptive advertising. The investigation covered both WeddingWire and The Knot, because they're the same entity. Use the reviews; be skeptical of the rankings.

The budget tool is an estimator, not a ledger. You can enter your total budget and get category allocation suggestions. You can't track actual payments, deposits, or outstanding balances. If your caterer is taking a deposit and you want to see how that affects your running total, WeddingWire can't do that.

## Why Kaiplan Exists

We built Kaiplan because no major wedding planning platform gives couples a real financial picture of their planning. The Knot, WeddingWire, and Zola all have budgets tools that are calculators or estimators. None of them track real spending.

We also wanted to build something that wasn't structurally motivated to push you toward vendors. No lead fees, no advertising. The only commercial relationship is with you.

Kaiplan's budget ledger records actual contracts, deposits paid, and outstanding balances. Vendor management tracks who you've hired, how to contact them, what you've signed, and what you still owe. Seating charts work. These are the features the advertising-based platforms don't prioritize because they don't generate revenue.

Honest caveat: Kaiplan doesn't have a vendor directory. You'll find vendors through WeddingWire, The Knot, Instagram, and word-of-mouth. Then manage those relationships in Kaiplan.

## The Practical Split

For most couples, a combination works: use WeddingWire (or The Knot — same thing) to discover vendors in your area. The directory is useful for that. Once you've found vendors, manage those relationships and your budget in Kaiplan.
