## The Name Confusion Is Real

Withjoy and Joy are not the same company. The name overlap trips up couples constantly — search results, Reddit threads, and review sites often conflate the two. The short version: withjoy.com is Withjoy, a separate wedding platform. joy.app is Joy, a different product. They share no ownership, no data, no integration.

This matters practically because reviews of one often get attributed to the other. Read reviews carefully and make sure the URL matches what you're evaluating.

## What Withjoy Offers

Withjoy is a wedding website builder with RSVP tools, hotel block management, and travel information pages. The hotel block feature is the main thing Withjoy offers that Joy doesn't — you can list your room block details, let guests request rooms, and track hotel RSVPs alongside event RSVPs.

That's useful for destination weddings or weddings where most guests are traveling. The execution has had problems though: Trustpilot reviews include documented cases of guests being charged for hotel reservations that were never confirmed. If you use this feature, research recent reviews before relying on it for anything financial.

The $69/year price is clear. No per-feature charges, no commissions, no vendor marketplace. That transparency is a genuine positive.

## What Joy Offers

Joy built its product around the guest experience. The dedicated mobile app is the centerpiece — guests download it, find your wedding, and get push notifications when you post updates, change the schedule, or share photos. For guests who aren't checking email frequently, this is more reliable than any web-based system.

The RSVP tools are the best in this tier. Automated reminders to non-responders (email and text), meal selection tracking, and clean couple-facing reporting make RSVP management less of a manual slog. Joy's free tier covers most of what couples need — the ~$39/event upgrade is for premium templates and customization.

## Planning Depth: Both Fall Short

Neither platform helps with the operational side of wedding planning. No budget tracking. No vendor management. No seating charts in any serious sense. Both are guest-facing tools that stop there.

For the couples who discover this late, it means building a spreadsheet alongside whatever platform they picked — or switching to something with more depth.

## The Clear Winner

Joy is the better choice for most couples. The guest app is a real feature that guests actually use. The RSVP tools are polished. The free tier is generous. And the hotel billing complaints on Withjoy's Trustpilot page are concerning enough to warrant caution unless that specific feature is the reason you're considering it.

Withjoy's hotel block tool is a differentiator for destination weddings — if that's a hard requirement and you've confirmed the current state of the feature from recent reviews, it's worth considering. Otherwise, Joy covers more ground with fewer reported issues.
