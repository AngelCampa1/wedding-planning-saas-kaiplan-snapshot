## How to Build Your Getting-Ready Timeline

Start with your ceremony time and work backwards. Every minute counts in the morning.

### Time Calculation Formula

**Step 1: Identify your hard deadline.**
This is the time you need to be at the ceremony venue, fully dressed, ready for photographs. For most weddings, this is 60-90 minutes before the ceremony start time.

**Hard deadline:** \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ AM

**Step 2: Subtract getting-dressed time.**
Allow 30-45 minutes for the couple to dress, including dress fastening, touch-ups, and last-minute items.

**Dress time starts:** \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ AM

**Step 3: Calculate total chair time.**

| Number of People Getting Services | Hours of Service Time (at 45-60 min each) |
|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| 3 people | 3-4 hours |
| 4 people | 4-5 hours |
| 5 people | 5-6 hours |
| 6 people | 6-7 hours |
| 7+ people | Consider a second stylist |

Add 30 minutes buffer for late starts, breaks, and transitions.

**Hair and makeup start time:** \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ AM

**Step 4: Confirm with your stylist.**
Share this timeline with your stylist before the wedding day, not on the morning of. If their timeline differs from yours, work it out in advance. On the morning of the wedding, the schedule is fixed.

## Getting-Ready Location Logistics

**Getting-ready location:**
- [ ] Hotel room (confirm room size and lighting; request a room with good natural light if possible)
- [ ] Venue bridal suite (confirm access time: available from \_\_\_\_\_ AM)
- [ ] Other location: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

### Hotel Room Considerations

- [ ] Confirm the hotel room is available from \_\_\_\_\_ AM (sometimes requires paying for an early check-in or booking the room for the night before)
- [ ] Request a high floor room with good natural light if possible
- [ ] Arrange for a full-length mirror if the room does not have one
- [ ] Book a second room for overflow if your party is large (stylists need space to move)
- [ ] Confirm parking for the stylist team

### Venue Bridal Suite Considerations

- [ ] Confirm access time with the venue coordinator
- [ ] Ask whether the bridal suite has adequate outlets for styling tools
- [ ] Confirm the venue has full-length mirrors
- [ ] Ask whether there is a separate space for the getting-ready photos (a cluttered suite does not photograph well)
- [ ] Confirm there is a dedicated getting-ready bathroom vs. a shared bathroom with other event spaces

## What to Pack in Your Getting-Ready Bag

Check off each item the night before the wedding. Pack everything in one bag, not scattered across multiple bags.

### Documents and Day-of Essentials

- [ ] Marriage license
- [ ] Vows (on a printed card)
- [ ] Vendor payment envelopes (label each with vendor name and amount)
- [ ] Day-of timeline (printed copy)
- [ ] Gratuity envelopes
- [ ] Phone charger
- [ ] Camera or memory card (if using personal camera for getting-ready photos)

### Beauty and Attire

- [ ] Wedding dress (confirm who is transporting it)
- [ ] Shoes
- [ ] All jewelry: earrings, necklace, bracelet, rings
- [ ] Veil or hair accessories
- [ ] Undergarments and shapewear
- [ ] Something old, something new, something borrowed, something blue (if applicable)
- [ ] Steamer (or confirm venue/hotel has one)
- [ ] Hangers that are photo-friendly (white or wooden velvet hangers)

### Touch-Up Kit (for during the day)

- [ ] Lip color matching your wedding makeup
- [ ] Blotting papers
- [ ] Setting spray
- [ ] Clear nail polish (for runs in hosiery or minor touch-ups)
- [ ] Compact mirror
- [ ] Mascara (waterproof)
- [ ] Dry shampoo

### Emergency Kit (for when things go wrong)

- [ ] Safety pins (at least 10, multiple sizes)
- [ ] Double-sided fashion tape
- [ ] Stain remover pen (Tide To Go or equivalent)
- [ ] Fabric tape
- [ ] Blister pads and bandages
- [ ] Pain reliever (ibuprofen and acetaminophen)
- [ ] Antacids
- [ ] Breath mints
- [ ] Sewing kit (needle, white thread, clear thread, scissors)
- [ ] Floss
- [ ] Bobby pins (50+)
- [ ] Hairspray travel size
- [ ] Straws (for drinking without disturbing lipstick)
- [ ] Snacks (crackers, nuts, something substantial for whoever forgets to eat)
- [ ] Phone battery pack / portable charger

## Day Before the Wedding

The night before is as important as the morning of. A few things done the evening before save you significant stress in the morning.

- [ ] Steam or press the wedding dress (do this the day before, not the morning of)
- [ ] Lay out all accessories and confirm nothing is missing
- [ ] Pack the getting-ready bag completely
- [ ] Confirm stylist arrival time via text
- [ ] Confirm transportation to the ceremony venue and who is driving
- [ ] Set multiple alarms for the morning
- [ ] Eat a real dinner (not just appetizers at the rehearsal dinner)
- [ ] Drink enough water
- [ ] Sleep in a comfortable bun or braid if you had a blowout, to protect the style
- [ ] Do not use any new skincare products or face treatments tonight
- [ ] Have all vendor payment envelopes ready and labeled
