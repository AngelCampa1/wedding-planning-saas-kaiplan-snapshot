## Per-Destination Cost Benchmarks

These are approximate ranges for a 7-10 day trip for two people in 2025-2026. Prices vary significantly by season, property tier, and how you book.

### All-Inclusive Beach Resort (Caribbean, Mexico)

| Item | Budget | Mid-Range | Luxury |
|------|--------|-----------|--------|
| Flights (roundtrip, 2 people) | $400-800 | $600-1,200 | $1,200-3,000+ (business) |
| Resort (all-inclusive, 7 nights) | $2,000-3,500 | $3,500-6,000 | $6,000-15,000+ |
| Excursions and activities | $200-400 | $400-800 | $800-2,000+ |
| Transportation (airport transfers) | $50-100 | $100-200 | $200-400 |
| **Approximate total** | $3,000-5,000 | $5,000-9,000 | $10,000-20,000+ |

**Most popular all-inclusive honeymoon destinations:** Cancun/Riviera Maya (Mexico), Punta Cana (Dominican Republic), Jamaica, St. Lucia, Turks and Caicos

**What "all-inclusive" typically includes:** Meals, drinks (house brands), non-motorized water sports, entertainment, and resort amenities.  
**What is usually NOT included:** Excursions off-property, premium alcohol, spa services, room service, tips (tipping culture varies by resort).

## Honeymoon Registry vs. Self-Funding

| Factor | Registry | Self-Funding |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| Control over booking | Less (timing depends on contributions) | Full control |
| Guest experience | Guests give experiences, not objects | Guests give cash (less personal feel) |
| Platform fees | 2-3% on contributions (most platforms) | No fees |
| Best for | Couples with specific experiences in mind | Couples with significant points or savings |

**Hybrid approach:** Self-fund the flights and hotel (where timing matters most), use a registry for specific experiences and upgrades guests can "gift."

## Credit Card Points Strategy for Honeymoon

If you are more than 6 months from the wedding and have not yet opened a travel credit card, this is worth doing.

### Basic Strategy

1. Open a travel rewards card after your engagement (good for 12-18 months of accumulation before the honeymoon).
2. Use it for all wedding vendor deposits and payments (venue, catering, photographer, florist, DJ).
3. Pay it off monthly. Points only have value if you are not paying interest.
4. Convert points to airline miles or hotel points before booking.

### Card Categories to Consider

**Flexible points (most versatile):** Chase Sapphire Preferred/Reserve, Amex Gold/Platinum, Capital One Venture. Points transfer to multiple airline and hotel programs.

**Hotel-branded cards:** Marriott Bonvoy, Hilton Honors, Hyatt. Best if you know you will stay at that hotel chain.

**Airline-branded cards:** Best if you have a specific airline in mind for the destination.

**Points calculation example:**
- $25,000 in wedding expenses on a 2x points card = 50,000 points
- 50,000 Chase points transferred to United Airlines = approximately $625-750 in flight value (at 1.25-1.5 cents/point)
- Same points on Hyatt = 1-2 free nights at a mid-tier property

## Travel Insurance Coverage Checklist

Before purchasing, confirm your policy covers these scenarios:

**Trip cancellation/interruption:**
- [ ] Illness or injury to traveler
- [ ] Illness or injury to immediate family member (remaining at home)
- [ ] Death in the family
- [ ] Severe weather making the destination inaccessible
- [ ] Airline or cruise line bankruptcy

**Medical coverage abroad:**
- [ ] Emergency medical treatment (confirm coverage amount: minimum $100,000 recommended)
- [ ] Emergency medical evacuation (minimum $250,000 recommended for remote destinations)
- [ ] Dental emergency coverage

**Other coverage:**
- [ ] Baggage loss or delay
- [ ] Travel delay (daily allowance for meals/hotel if flight is delayed 6+ hours)
- [ ] Cancel for any reason (CFAR) upgrade — costs 40-50% more but covers situations standard policies do not

**Where to buy:** Travel insurance is available directly from insurers (Allianz, AXA, Travel Guard) or aggregators (InsureMyTrip, Squaremouth). Compare at least 3 policies before purchasing.

## Packing and Practical Tips

**For beach/resort destinations:**
- Bring sunscreen from home; resort shops charge 3-5x normal prices.
- Pack a small dry bag for beach and water activities.
- Check whether your resort provides beach towels or charges for them.
- Most resorts allow one carry-on and one checked bag per person; check before booking if you are packing heavy.

**For city trips:**
- A portable phone charger is essential for full days of navigation.
- Bring a small crossbody bag or money belt for walking; pickpocketing is common in tourist areas in many European cities.
- Notify your bank and credit card company of travel dates and destinations before you leave.

**For adventure/remote destinations:**
- Get travel vaccinations 4-6 weeks before departure (some require multiple doses).
- Carry prescription medications in your carry-on, never checked luggage.
- Confirm your health insurance coverage abroad and purchase supplemental medical coverage if it is limited.
