## 12+ Months Before the Wedding

This phase is about locking in the high-demand vendors before dates disappear. Anything with a limited supply per date (venues, photographers, bands) must be booked here.

**Budget and Vision**
- [ ] Decide on a rough total budget with both partners (and any contributing family members)
- [ ] Agree on rough guest count (this drives almost every other budget decision)
- [ ] Define priorities: what is most important to you both? (Food, photography, music, flowers, etc.)
- [ ] Open a dedicated wedding savings account or track wedding spending separately
- [ ] Set up a simple budget spreadsheet or use Kaiplan's budget module

**Date and Venue**
- [ ] Agree on a target month or season
- [ ] Research venues in your area (start with 5-8 options)
- [ ] Tour at least 3-5 venues using a comparison worksheet
- [ ] Book your venue and sign the contract
- [ ] Confirm your wedding date

**Vendors (Book Immediately After Venue)**
- [ ] Research and contact photographers (view 3+ portfolios, check availability)
- [ ] Book your photographer and sign the contract
- [ ] Research and contact videographers (if desired)
- [ ] Book your videographer and sign the contract
- [ ] Research caterers (if not provided by venue)
- [ ] Schedule tastings with top catering options
- [ ] Research and book an officiant

**Wedding Party**
- [ ] Ask your bridal party/wedding party (have the conversations before formal invitations go out)
- [ ] Confirm wedding party participation with everyone

## 9-12 Months Before the Wedding

This phase covers the secondary vendors and the guest list infrastructure. You should have your venue locked and your date confirmed before starting this section.

**Vendors**
- [ ] Book caterer (if not venue-included)
- [ ] Research and book a DJ or band
- [ ] Begin florist research (look at portfolios, get quotes from 2-3 florists)
- [ ] Book florist
- [ ] Research hair and makeup artists (look at bridal portfolio specifically)
- [ ] Book hair and makeup team

**Attire**
- [ ] Begin wedding dress shopping (order lead times are 4-6 months for custom/designer)
- [ ] Purchase or order wedding dress
- [ ] Research attire for groom/partner and wedding party
- [ ] Order or confirm wedding party attire

**Guest List and Save the Dates**
- [ ] Finalize the guest list with full names and addresses
- [ ] Set the RSVP deadline (should be 3 weeks before the wedding)
- [ ] Design and order save the dates
- [ ] Mail save the dates (12 months out for destination weddings, 9 months for local)

**Honeymoon**
- [ ] Research honeymoon destinations and set a honeymoon budget
- [ ] Book flights, hotels, or resort packages
- [ ] Confirm passport validity (must be valid 6 months beyond return date for most countries)
- [ ] Research visa requirements if international

**Logistics**
- [ ] Reserve hotel room blocks for out-of-town guests
- [ ] Share hotel block information with guests when you send save the dates

## 6-9 Months Before the Wedding

This phase is about invitations, details, and the decisions that require the most lead time (custom orders, travel, legal name change preparation).

**Invitations**
- [ ] Design invitations (hire a designer or use a template)
- [ ] Order invitations, RSVP cards, and envelopes
- [ ] Compile full mailing addresses for all guests
- [ ] Purchase postage (weigh a complete invite package first; wedding invites often need extra postage)
- [ ] Stuff and mail invitations (8 weeks before the wedding for domestic, 10-12 for destination)

**Vendors and Details**
- [ ] Meet with florist to finalize arrangements (bouquets, ceremony, reception centerpieces)
- [ ] Meet with DJ or band to discuss playlist, must-plays, and do-not-plays
- [ ] Schedule wedding cake/dessert tasting and book baker
- [ ] Book any additional day-of vendors: transportation, photo booth, etc.
- [ ] Book ceremony musician(s) if different from reception music

**Logistics**
- [ ] Research wedding insurance options and purchase if applicable
- [ ] Confirm hotel block details and provide booking instructions to guests
- [ ] Plan rehearsal dinner (date, venue, guest list)
- [ ] Book rehearsal dinner venue

**Preparation**
- [ ] Schedule engagement photo session with photographer
- [ ] Begin thinking about wedding ceremony structure (vows, readings, rituals)
- [ ] Research marriage license requirements in your specific county

## 3-6 Months Before the Wedding

This is where planning accelerates. Most couples feel the most stress in this window because decisions are becoming final and the guest count is not yet confirmed.

**Vendors: Final Details**
- [ ] Schedule catering tasting (if not done) and finalize menu
- [ ] Order wedding cake and confirm delivery logistics
- [ ] Meet with photographer to go over shot list and timeline
- [ ] Confirm all vendor contracts are signed and deposits are paid
- [ ] Research and order wedding favors (if using)
- [ ] Order ceremony programs

**Attire**
- [ ] Schedule first dress fitting
- [ ] Confirm wedding party attire has been ordered/rented
- [ ] Purchase wedding shoes and begin breaking them in
- [ ] Research and purchase wedding jewelry and accessories

**Guest Management**
- [ ] Track RSVPs as they come in (use a spreadsheet or Kaiplan's guest list tool)
- [ ] Follow up on any late RSVPs 1 week after the RSVP deadline
- [ ] Finalize headcount for caterer estimate

**Legal and Administrative**
- [ ] Begin legal name change research (Social Security, passport, driver's license) if applicable
- [ ] Research marriage license office hours and appointment requirements

## 1-3 Months Before the Wedding

The details phase. Logistics and vendor communication become the primary work.

**Fittings and Alterations**
- [ ] Second dress fitting
- [ ] Final dress fitting (4 weeks out)
- [ ] Pick up dress and wedding party attire

**Vendors: Final Confirmations**
- [ ] Send detailed day-of timeline to all vendors
- [ ] Confirm arrival times with every vendor
- [ ] Confirm payment method and final invoice amounts
- [ ] Prepare payment envelopes (cash or check for day-of gratuities)

**Legal**
- [ ] Obtain marriage license (check your county's timing requirements)
- [ ] Confirm officiant has all required information for the ceremony

**Guest Management**
- [ ] Finalize RSVP count and submit to caterer
- [ ] Confirm meal choices with caterer (if applicable)
- [ ] Begin seating chart draft

**Ceremony**
- [ ] Write or finalize personal vows (if using)
- [ ] Rehearse vows out loud, not just in your head
- [ ] Confirm ceremony structure with officiant

## 1 Month Before the Wedding

This month is about final confirmations and building the master timeline.

**Seating and Logistics**
- [ ] Finalize seating chart
- [ ] Create and print place cards
- [ ] Print or finalize ceremony programs

**Timeline**
- [ ] Build the complete day-of timeline (from hair/makeup start to reception end)
- [ ] Share timeline with wedding party and immediate family
- [ ] Share timeline with all vendors
- [ ] Confirm rehearsal logistics and timing

**Financial**
- [ ] Make all remaining vendor payments by their due dates
- [ ] Prepare gratuity envelopes (label each with vendor name)
- [ ] Confirm who will distribute gratuity envelopes on the wedding day

**Practical**
- [ ] Break in wedding shoes (wear them at home for 30-minute sessions)
- [ ] Schedule blowout/hair appointment the day before if desired
- [ ] Arrange for someone to pick up and return the dress after the wedding
- [ ] Plan for rings: confirm their location, who holds them, when they are given to the ring bearer

## Week of the Wedding

- [ ] Confirm all vendor arrival times one final time (a brief email or text is sufficient)
- [ ] Confirm rehearsal dinner details with attendees
- [ ] Attend rehearsal and go through ceremony order once
- [ ] Host rehearsal dinner
- [ ] Confirm hotel accommodations for wedding night
- [ ] Pick up wedding dress if not already done
- [ ] Prepare an emergency kit (safety pins, double-sided tape, stain remover pen, blister pads, pain relievers, clear nail polish)
- [ ] Charge all devices (phones, cameras, anything battery-powered)
- [ ] Delegate day-of tasks to specific people (who is managing gratuities, who has the rings, who helps guests with parking)
- [ ] Review the day-of timeline one final time
- [ ] Pack a personal getting-ready bag (dress, accessories, vows, phone charger, snacks)

## Wedding Day

**Morning**
- [ ] Eat breakfast (couples skip this and regret it)
- [ ] Getting ready starts on schedule per the beauty timeline
- [ ] Marriage license is with the officiant or designated person
- [ ] Rings are confirmed with ring bearer or best person
- [ ] Vows are confirmed (physical copy or phone, in someone's pocket)

**Ceremony**
- [ ] All vendors confirmed on-site and set up
- [ ] Wedding party briefed on processional order
- [ ] Ceremony starts within 5-10 minutes of scheduled time

**Reception**
- [ ] Gratuity envelopes distributed to coordinator before reception
- [ ] Photographer captures key reception moments per shot list
- [ ] First dance, parent dances, toasts per timeline
- [ ] Cake cutting per catering team's signal

**End of Night**
- [ ] Confirm who is taking the cake top home (for anniversary tradition, if applicable)
- [ ] Confirm who is collecting gifts and cards
- [ ] Personal items gathered from getting-ready suite
- [ ] Confirm all vendors have their signed contracts/tips

## After the Wedding

- [ ] Send thank-you notes (aim to mail within 3 months)
- [ ] Submit marriage license for filing if the officiant did not file it
- [ ] Begin legal name change process if applicable
- [ ] Return any rented items (suits, decor, etc.)
- [ ] Review and back up photos when delivered
- [ ] Write vendor reviews for photographers, caterers, and others who served you well
- [ ] Cancel or freeze any vendors you did not end up using (confirm nothing is still on autopay)
