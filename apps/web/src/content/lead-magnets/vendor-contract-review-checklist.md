## Universal Checklist: Apply to Every Vendor Contract

Work through this list before you review any vendor-specific section. These clauses appear in nearly every wedding contract and are the source of most disputes.

### Payment Terms

- [ ] Deposit amount is specified as a dollar figure (not just a percentage): $\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
- [ ] Deposit due date is specified: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
- [ ] Final balance amount is specified: $\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
- [ ] Final balance due date is specified: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (confirm this is before the wedding, not after)
- [ ] Accepted payment methods are listed (check, credit card, wire transfer, cash)
- [ ] Any credit card processing fee is disclosed
- [ ] Late payment fee or consequence is specified (or confirmed absent)

### Cancellation and Refund Policy

- [ ] Cancellation procedure is specified (written notice required, by what method?)
- [ ] Refund schedule based on cancellation timing is present:
  - [ ] Cancellation more than 12 months out: refund terms = \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
  - [ ] Cancellation 6-12 months out: refund terms = \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
  - [ ] Cancellation under 6 months: refund terms = \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
  - [ ] Cancellation under 30 days: refund terms = \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
- [ ] Is the deposit refundable under any circumstances? Under what conditions?
- [ ] What happens to payments already made if the vendor cancels on you?

### Deliverables and Scope

- [ ] Specific hours of coverage or service are stated: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ hours
- [ ] Start time and end time are specified (not just "duration"): \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ to \_\_\_\_\_\_\_
- [ ] Everything included in the package is listed explicitly (not just a package name)
- [ ] What is NOT included is clear (or you have asked and received a written answer)
- [ ] Any add-ons or upgrades discussed verbally are included in the written contract

### Overtime

- [ ] Overtime rate per hour is specified: $\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ / hour
- [ ] Process for requesting overtime on the day is clear (does the vendor decide or does the couple?)
- [ ] Maximum overtime hours (if any) is specified

### Substitution Policy

- [ ] The contract specifies who specifically will perform the service (name, not just company)
- [ ] What happens if the named individual is unavailable (illness, emergency) is addressed
- [ ] Does the couple have approval rights over a substitute?
- [ ] If a substitute is used, is there any pricing adjustment?

### Force Majeure

- [ ] A force majeure clause exists
- [ ] The clause specifies what qualifies (acts of God, government orders, natural disasters)
- [ ] What happens to the deposit in a force majeure scenario is specified
- [ ] Whether the couple can reschedule vs. cancel in a force majeure event is addressed

### Insurance and Liability

- [ ] The vendor carries general liability insurance
- [ ] Coverage amount is specified or can be confirmed on request
- [ ] Who is responsible for damage to venue during vendor's work is addressed

## Photographer Contract: Additional Checklist

- [ ] The specific photographer shooting the wedding is named in the contract
- [ ] Number of hours of coverage is specified and matches what you discussed
- [ ] Whether a second shooter is included or an add-on is specified
- [ ] Engagement session is included or excluded (note: if included, it often has its own expiration date)
- [ ] Image delivery format is specified (digital files, print release, or both)
- [ ] Image delivery timeline is specified: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ weeks after the wedding
- [ ] Minimum number of edited images to be delivered is specified: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ images
- [ ] Whether RAW files are included is addressed (most photographers do not include RAW files)
- [ ] Editing style is described (light and airy, true-to-life, dark and moody) and matches portfolio
- [ ] Whether the photographer can use your images for portfolio/marketing is addressed (and whether you can opt out)
- [ ] Album or print products: if ordered, pricing and delivery timeline is specified
- [ ] Copyright and usage rights: what the couple can do with the digital files (printing, sharing, commercial use)
- [ ] Exclusivity clause: does the contract prevent other professional photographers or video teams?

## Caterer Contract: Additional Checklist

- [ ] Per-person food cost is specified with exact menu (not just package name)
- [ ] Service charge percentage is stated: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_%
- [ ] Tax rate is stated: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_%
- [ ] Minimum guest count guarantee is specified: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ guests
- [ ] Overage policy (if actual guests exceed guarantee): $\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ per person over
- [ ] Final headcount due date is specified: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
- [ ] Bar package is specified (open bar, beer/wine only, non-alcoholic only, cash bar)
- [ ] Alcohol-related liability coverage is addressed
- [ ] Setup and breakdown included in service, or charged separately
- [ ] Staffing ratio or number of servers is specified
- [ ] What happens to leftover food is addressed (can couples take it? Does caterer keep it?)
- [ ] Vendor meal requirement is specified (how many meals for the vendor team, at what cost)

---

## Venue Contract: Additional Checklist

- [ ] Rental period (start and end time, including vendor setup and breakdown) is specified
- [ ] Exclusive venue use vs. shared events on the same day is addressed
- [ ] In-house vs. outside vendor policy for catering is specified
- [ ] Food and beverage minimum (if applicable): $\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (confirm if service charge counts toward this)
- [ ] Outside vendor fee (if applicable): $\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ per outside vendor
- [ ] Preferred vendor list requirements are fully disclosed
- [ ] Noise curfew is specified: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ PM
- [ ] Amplified music restrictions (outdoor only after certain hours?) are specified
- [ ] Décor restrictions are listed (candles, confetti, hanging items, rice/petals)
- [ ] Parking: number of spaces, whether valet is required, any cost to guests
- [ ] Venue coordinator: included or charged separately, what they cover
- [ ] Required liability insurance coverage amount: $\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
- [ ] Rain or weather backup plan is described in the contract

---

## DJ or Band Contract: Additional Checklist

- [ ] Specific DJ or band name and lead performer are specified
- [ ] Hours of performance are specified (distinct from setup/breakdown time)
- [ ] Setup and breakdown time (before performance) is specified and who covers it
- [ ] Equipment list is specified (PA system, lighting, mics for toasts, photo booth, etc.)
- [ ] Music library access: do they have the songs on your must-play list?
- [ ] Do-not-play list: is there a mechanism for submitting this?
- [ ] MC duties are specified (announcing entrance, toasts, dances)
- [ ] Whether additional equipment (uplighting, photo booth) is included or priced separately
- [ ] Exclusivity clause: can guests plug in phones to play music? (Some DJ contracts prohibit this)
- [ ] If the DJ is ill, what is the backup plan?

---

## Florist Contract: Additional Checklist

- [ ] Every arrangement is listed by name and approximate size/style
- [ ] Flower types are specified (or noted as "subject to seasonal availability with substitutions of equal value")
- [ ] Delivery time and location are specified
- [ ] Setup is included or charged separately
- [ ] Who is responsible if flowers wilt or are damaged in transit
- [ ] Rental items (vases, arches, stands) vs. items you keep: each arrangement is labeled
- [ ] Rental item return process and timeline is specified
- [ ] What happens if specific flowers are unavailable: substitution approval process

## Red Flags in Any Contract

Stop and investigate further if you see any of the following:

- **No specific names.** Any contract that says "a qualified professional" instead of naming the specific person who will be at your wedding.
- **Vague deliverables.** "Full-day photography coverage" with no hour minimum, no image count, no delivery timeline.
- **All deposits non-refundable under all circumstances.** Some non-refundable deposit is normal. 100% non-refundable on the entire contract balance, including amounts paid after signing, is not.
- **No mention of what happens if the vendor cancels.** A contract that is entirely one-sided regarding cancellation penalties.
- **Unlimited substitution rights.** The vendor can send anyone they want, with no notice or approval from you.
- **No liability for their own errors.** Language that caps the vendor's liability at the amount paid, regardless of the nature of the failure.

If any of these appear, ask for the clause to be revised before signing. A vendor who refuses to put specific names or delivery timelines in writing is telling you something about how they operate.
