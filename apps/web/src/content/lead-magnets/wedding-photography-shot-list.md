## How to Fill Out This Shot List

Print this template and work through it with your partner 2-3 weeks before the wedding. Highlight the must-have shots, cross out anything irrelevant to your wedding, and fill in every blank in the Family Formals section with actual names. Give the completed list to your photographer at your final planning meeting, not on the wedding day.

**Timing overview for planning purposes:**

| Section | Estimated Time |
|---------|---------------|
| Getting ready (full coverage) | 90-120 min |
| First look (if applicable) | 20-30 min |
| Ceremony | 30-60 min |
| Family formals (10 groupings) | 35-45 min |
| Wedding party photos | 20-30 min |
| Couple portraits (golden hour) | 20-30 min |
| Reception (entrance through exit) | 3-4 hours |

## Ceremony

**Timing note:** Communicate the ceremony start time and any processional order changes to your photographer at least one week before the wedding. Confirm where they can stand during the ceremony (some officiants restrict movement during the ceremony).

### Pre-Ceremony

- [ ] Venue exterior (establishing shot)
- [ ] Ceremony space (empty or nearly empty, before guests arrive)
- [ ] Ceremony details: altar flowers, candles, programs, reserved seating signs
- [ ] Guests arriving and being seated
- [ ] Officiant taking position
- [ ] Groom/partner(s) walking out with family or entering with wedding party

### Processional

- [ ] Each wedding party member walking down the aisle (both angles if possible)
- [ ] Flower girl(s): \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ and/or ring bearer(s): \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
- [ ] Bride/partner entering (wide shot from the front showing the full aisle)
- [ ] Reaction shot of groom/waiting partner seeing bride enter
- [ ] Parent(s) walking the bride (if applicable): \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
- [ ] Guests rising and turning to watch
- [ ] Couple together at the altar (wide shot)

### Ceremony Moments

- [ ] Readings (reader at the podium, couple listening)
- [ ] Exchange of vows (both partners, close enough to capture expression)
- [ ] Ring exchange (hands close-up)
- [ ] Unity ceremony (candle lighting, sand, handfasting, etc.): \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
- [ ] "You may now kiss" moment (wide and close shot)
- [ ] Reaction of guests and wedding party to the kiss
- [ ] Signing of the marriage license (if happening at the ceremony)

### Recessional

- [ ] Couple walking back up the aisle together (their expressions, not posed)
- [ ] Guests celebrating as couple passes
- [ ] Wedding party recessional
- [ ] Guests exiting and gathering outside

## Family Formals

**Timing note:** Shoot family formals immediately after the ceremony while everyone is together and still dressed. 3-4 minutes per grouping. Have a family member (not the couple) organize and call names.

**How to fill out your list:** Write the names of each person in every grouping below. If a name is blank, that combination will be skipped. Add rows as needed. Work from largest group to smallest so you can release family members as you finish their combinations.
