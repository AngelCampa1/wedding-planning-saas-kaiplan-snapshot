## When to Book and Why Waiting Is Risky

The biggest honeymoon planning mistake is treating it as a post-wedding task. By the time your wedding is three weeks out, your time and attention are consumed by vendor confirmations, seating charts, and a hundred small decisions. Honeymoon planning requires research and decisions that deserve real attention, not a rushed weekend on your laptop between vendor calls.

Book your flights and accommodation at least 6 months before travel for domestic trips. For international trips, particularly to destinations with limited inventory (the Maldives, Bora Bora, Amalfi Coast, popular Japan ryokans), 9-12 months is not excessive.

**Why early booking matters:**

Honeymoon-specific room categories (overwater bungalows in the Maldives, suite upgrades at Italian hotels, private villas in Bali) are limited inventory items. They do not get better as the travel date approaches. They fill up, and what remains is either unavailable or priced at a premium.

Flight prices are less predictable, but international routes frequently have better availability and pricing 4-6 months out than they do in the 60 days before departure.

Most couples book their honeymoon during the same stretch when they are actively wedding planning, roughly 9-12 months out. That means honeymoon research is competing for the same time and mental energy as venue tours and photographer meetings, which is why it keeps getting pushed. Put a specific date on your calendar for it. A solid international honeymoon takes about 10-15 hours of research and decision-making to sort out properly.

## Budget Breakdown

The average spend of $4,000-$5,000 breaks down roughly like this for a 7-10 day trip:

**Flights:** $800-$2,000 per couple for international (varies enormously by destination and booking timing); $300-$800 domestic.

**Accommodation:** $150-$400 per night for mid-range hotels; $400-$1,000+ per night for luxury resorts. A 7-night stay at a nice-but-not-ultra-luxury resort runs $1,050-$2,800.

**Food:** $100-$200 per day per couple depending on destination and dining choices.

**Activities:** $500-$1,500 depending on what you plan: snorkeling tours, cooking classes, guided excursions, private transfers.

**Travel insurance:** $150-$350.

**Miscellaneous (tips, bags, incidentals):** $300-$500.

At the $4,500 average, most couples are doing a 7-night trip with mid-range accommodation, two or three activities, and a mix of restaurant meals. A week in Bali, Portugal, Mexico, or Costa Rica fits comfortably at this price point. A week in the Maldives or Bora Bora does not. Those destinations run $500-$2,000 per night at resorts where that price point is standard.

## Destination Categories With Real Comparisons

### Beach and Resort

**Maldives:** Most expensive category. Overwater bungalows start at $600-$800 per night and go to $2,000+. Almost all resorts are all-inclusive or require add-on packages for every meal. A 7-night trip commonly costs $8,000-$15,000 per couple. The experience is visually stunning and genuinely private. The cost is real.

**Bora Bora:** Similar to the Maldives in price and category. French Polynesia's tax structure and remoteness (you fly through Tahiti) drives costs up. Budget $8,000-$12,000 for 7 nights.

**Hawaii:** $5,000-$8,000 for a 7-night trip from the US mainland including flights. Maui and Kauai are the top honeymoon islands. No passport required. This is the premium US domestic option.

**Caribbean:** Wide range. St. Barts and Turks and Caicos are luxury-priced ($6,000-$10,000+). Jamaica, Dominican Republic, and Mexico's Caribbean coast (Tulum, Playa del Carmen) run $3,000-$5,000 for a solid trip.

### Cultural and City

**Italy:** The most popular honeymoon destination among US couples. Rome, Amalfi Coast, Tuscany, and Venice are the main draws. Flights from the US run $800-$1,400. Hotels vary enormously. A 10-day Italy trip with a mix of cities and countryside can be done for $4,000-$7,000 depending on accommodation choices. Shoulder season (April-May, September-October) is better weather and lower prices than summer.

**Japan:** Increasingly popular. Tokyo and Kyoto are the starting point; many couples add Hiroshima or a ryokan stay. Flights run $1,000-$1,400 from the West Coast, $1,200-$1,600 from the East Coast. In-country rail is excellent and efficient. A 10-day trip runs $4,000-$7,000.

**Portugal:** Underpriced relative to quality. Lisbon and the Alentejo wine region are excellent. Flights are cheaper than most European destinations from the US. A 10-day trip including flights runs $3,000-$5,000 for mid-range accommodation.

### Adventure and Active

**Costa Rica:** Zip-lining, hiking, wildlife, beaches, and excellent boutique hotels. Shorter flight from the US than most international destinations. A 7-10 day trip runs $3,000-$5,000.

**New Zealand:** More expensive due to distance and the dollar exchange. $5,000-$8,000 for a 10-14 day trip. Best for couples who specifically want outdoor adventure.

**Iceland:** All-year destination (Northern Lights in winter, midnight sun in summer). Flights from the East Coast are direct and often reasonably priced ($500-$800 round trip). A 7-day trip runs $3,500-$6,000.

## What to Skip

These honeymoon additions rarely deliver value equal to their cost:

**Helicopter transfers:** $500-$1,500 per person for a 20-minute flight instead of a 45-minute boat or car ride. Memorable, but not essential.

**Multi-destination itineraries on short trips:** Spending 3 nights in Rome, 2 in Florence, and 2 in Amalfi sounds like a lot of Italy. It is also four nights checking in and out of hotels with luggage and travel days eating into your actual vacation time. Two well-chosen locations for a week each is better than six locations in the same time.

**Overbooked activity schedules:** One structured activity per day is usually enough. Two is a busy day. Three is a work trip. Leave margin for wandering, sleeping in, and eating slowly.

## Passport, Visa, and Name Change Logistics

If you are changing your name after the wedding, book travel in your pre-marriage name and update documents after the honeymoon. Airlines and customs match your ticket to your passport name. Any discrepancy can create boarding issues.

Check your passport's expiration date now. Most countries require at least six months of validity beyond your return date. A passport expiring in October and a honeymoon returning in July means you are fine. A passport expiring in July with a June return cuts it close and some airlines will not board you.

For countries that require visas (Vietnam, India, Australia, and others from the US), check current requirements and processing times at least 3 months before your trip. Some visas are electronic and issued in hours; others require mailed applications and weeks of processing.
