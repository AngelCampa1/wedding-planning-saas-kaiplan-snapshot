## The 45-55% Rule for Venue and Catering

At 100 guests, venue and catering are always the budget anchors. The practical ceiling: spend no more than 50-55% of your total budget on venue plus catering combined.

For a $35,000 total budget, that's $17,500-$19,250 for venue and food. That leaves $15,750-$17,500 for everything else.

The mistake: booking a venue with a $12,000 minimum and paying $18,000 all-in for catering, leaving only $12,000 for photography, music, florals, attire, and everything else. At that point, something meaningful gets cut.

Allocate before booking. The first two vendor commitments (venue and caterer) should be made knowing what's left in the budget for the remaining 8-10 categories.

## Where to Save at 100 Guests

### Music

A DJ ($1,500-$2,500) delivers a similar dance floor experience to a band ($4,000-$12,000) at a fraction of the cost. Unless live music is a specific priority, the DJ option is the clearest budget optimization.

### Florals

Greenery-forward arrangements cost 30-40% less than bloom-heavy designs. Repurposing ceremony florals into reception decor eliminates duplicate purchases.

### Off-peak date

Friday or Sunday weddings typically save $1,000-$3,000 on venue. January-March saves more.

### Catering service style

Buffet costs less than plated service due to lower staffing requirements. For 100 guests, the staffing difference between buffet and plated can be $2,000-$4,000.

### Cake alternative

A dessert table or cupcakes costs $3-$5/person vs. $8-$15 for tiered cake, savings of $300-$1,000 at 100 guests.
