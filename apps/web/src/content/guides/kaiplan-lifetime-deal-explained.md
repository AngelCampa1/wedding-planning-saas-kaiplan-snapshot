## What the Lifetime Deal Is

Kaiplan offers three pricing options:

- **Starter:** $20/month
- **Pro:** $35/month
- **Lifetime:** $100 one-time

The Lifetime plan gives you Pro-tier access permanently. You pay once and you're done. There's no annual renewal, no subscription to remember to cancel, and no recurring charge on your credit card.

This page explains exactly what's included, how the math works out compared to monthly plans, and who the Lifetime option makes the most sense for.

---

## What's Included in the Lifetime Plan

The Lifetime plan is not a stripped-down version of Pro. You get the same features:

**Budget tracker:** Full access to the budget tracking features including total budget, category budgets, vendor line items, deposit and balance tracking, payment due date visibility, and overage flagging.

**Vendor management:** Full vendor manager with contact information, booking status, contract notes, payment schedules, confirmation numbers, and vendor comparison view.

**Guest list:** Full guest list management including CSV import, RSVP tracking, dietary notes, plus-one management, and export.

**Planning checklist:** Full checklist with default milestones, custom task creation, task completion tracking, and timeline view.

**PWA access:** Accessible from any browser on any device (phone, tablet, laptop). No downloads required.

**Permanent access:** Your account does not expire after your wedding. Your planning data, budget records, and vendor information remain available.

## The Math: Lifetime vs. Monthly Plans

The break-even point for the Lifetime plan compared to a Pro monthly subscription is approximately 3 months (3 x $35 = $105). After 3 months of Pro, you've spent more than the Lifetime cost.

Here's the comparison for common engagement lengths:

| Engagement Length          | Monthly Starter Cost | Monthly Pro Cost | Lifetime Cost |
| -------------------------- | -------------------- | ---------------- | ------------- |
| 3 months                   | $60                  | $105             | $100          |
| 6 months                   | $120                 | $210             | $100          |
| 9 months                   | $180                 | $315             | $100          |
| 12 months                  | $240                 | $420             | $100          |
| 14 months (avg engagement) | $280                 | $490             | $100          |
| 18 months                  | $360                 | $630             | $100          |

The average US engagement is 14 months. At Pro monthly for 14 months, you'd pay $490. The Lifetime plan costs $100.

Even compared to the Starter plan ($20/month), the Lifetime plan pays back at the 5-month mark.

**The break-even point is the key question:** If you have 3 or more months of active planning ahead of you, the Lifetime plan is cheaper than Pro monthly. If you have 5 or more months, it's cheaper than Starter monthly.

## Who the Lifetime Plan Makes the Most Sense For

**Couples who just got engaged and have a year or more until the wedding.** This is the clearest case. If you have 12-18 months of planning ahead of you, the subscription cost adds up significantly. The Lifetime plan locks in your total cost at $100.

**Couples who want to start planning immediately.** Some couples want to start tracking vendors and building their budget before they've set a firm date. Starting early with a monthly plan means you're paying for months of planning that may not be intense. With Lifetime, you start when you're ready without worrying about the monthly meter running.

**Couples who want simplicity.** Some people just don't want to manage another subscription. The Lifetime plan removes that recurring charge from the equation entirely.

**Couples who plan to use the data after the wedding.** Some couples want to reference their budget, vendor contacts, and planning history after the event - for tax purposes, for reference if they're in the wedding industry, or just because they want the record. Lifetime access means the data stays available.

---

## Who the Lifetime Plan Is Less Compelling For

**Couples with very short planning windows (under 3 months).** If you're planning a wedding in 60-90 days, a monthly plan may be cheaper overall since you won't be using the tool for long. At 2 months of Starter, you'd pay $40 vs. $100 for Lifetime.

**Couples who aren't sure they'll use a planning tool at all.** If you're evaluating whether Kaiplan fits your workflow, start with a monthly plan. Try it for a month and see if it changes how you plan. Upgrade to Lifetime once you've confirmed the tool works for you.

**Couples who primarily need features Kaiplan doesn't have.** If you need vendor discovery, registry management, a native guest app, hotel block tools, or print invitations, those aren't included in any Kaiplan tier. Spending $100 on a tool that doesn't cover your primary need isn't the right move.

## Common Questions About the Lifetime Plan

**Is this a limited-time offer?**

The Lifetime plan pricing may change in the future as Kaiplan develops and the subscription value grows. We're not offering it as a "limited-time sale" with an artificial countdown - but pricing decisions for early buyers vs. later buyers are a normal part of how SaaS products evolve. If you're considering the Lifetime plan, the current $100 price is what you'd pay today.

**What happens if Kaiplan shuts down?**

This is a fair question to ask about any lifetime software purchase. Kaiplan is an active product being built by Angel Campa, a principal SDET building this as a validated SaaS. It's not a side project in maintenance mode. That said, no software company can guarantee indefinite operation. If you want maximum security, a monthly plan gives you flexibility to cancel at any time.

**Does Lifetime include new features as they ship?**

Yes. As new Pro features are added to Kaiplan, Lifetime members receive those features. We don't plan to gate new functionality behind an additional tier for existing Lifetime members.

**What's the refund policy?**

Check the current terms at kaiplan.app. We want people to feel confident in the purchase rather than feel locked in to something that doesn't work for them.

## Lifetime vs. Starter: Which Monthly Tier to Compare Against

Some couples will look at the Lifetime plan and compare it to the Starter monthly ($20/month) rather than Pro monthly ($35/month). That comparison is worth being direct about.

The Lifetime plan is priced as a Pro equivalent. If Starter is all you need, the monthly comparison looks like this:

- 5 months of Starter = $100 (Lifetime break-even at Starter pricing)
- 14-month engagement at Starter = $280

So even against Starter, the Lifetime plan pays back if you have more than 5 months of planning ahead.

The question of whether you need Starter features or Pro features is separate. Check the [pricing page](/pricing) for a feature comparison between tiers.
