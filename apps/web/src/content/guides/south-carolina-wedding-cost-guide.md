## How much does a wedding in South Carolina cost?

South Carolina is a state with a notable two-tier wedding market. Charleston has become one of the most sought-after destination wedding locations in the Southeast, drawing couples from across the country for its historic architecture, waterfront settings, and antebellum plantation venues. That demand has pushed Charleston wedding costs significantly above the state average.

Outside Charleston, South Carolina is an affordable state for weddings. Based on national data from The Knot's 2026 Real Weddings Study and regional market pricing, the estimated statewide average is around $27,000 — about 21% below the national average of $34,200.

That number blends Charleston's elevated market with more affordable markets in the Midlands (Columbia), the Upstate (Greenville, Spartanburg), and the Grand Strand (Myrtle Beach). If you're planning a Charleston wedding, the state average provides a misleading starting point. Budget for the Charleston market specifically — $35,000–$55,000 for a mid-tier event with 100 guests is realistic.

Heat and humidity are the primary seasonal planning factor in South Carolina. June through September runs hot enough that outdoor afternoon events are genuinely uncomfortable for guests. The popular wedding months are March through May and late September through November. October in particular is competitive across the state, especially in Charleston.

## Breaking down South Carolina wedding costs

The table above compares costs across the three main South Carolina wedding markets. A few things to note:

- **Charleston venue costs** include a wide range of venue types. Historic homes and plantation properties carry the highest fees and often come with required caterers that charge above-market rates. Hotel ballrooms in Charleston run lower on fees but can have comparable F&B minimums.
- **Florals** in South Carolina summer weddings require additional consideration. High heat shortens the viability window for delicate flowers. Many South Carolina florists add delivery logistics fees for summer weddings, and some couples choose tropical or hardier blooms for summer.
- **Catering** across the state benefits from competitive regional pricing. Low-country cuisine (shrimp and grits stations, oyster roasts, she-crab soup) is both popular and cost-effective in coastal markets.

## Most expensive vs. most affordable South Carolina markets

**Higher cost markets:**
- **Charleston / Mt. Pleasant** — By far the most expensive South Carolina market. Year-round destination demand keeps vendor rates elevated and prime dates booked long in advance.
- **Hilton Head** — Resort island with destination pricing comparable to Charleston for venue fees and accommodations.
- **Lake Keowee / Greenwood Lake area** — Lake resort venues charge premiums for waterfront access, particularly in summer.

**More affordable markets:**
- **Greenville** — The Upstate's largest city has a growing wedding market with prices well below Charleston. Downtown Greenville event spaces and nearby farm venues offer strong value.
- **Columbia** — The state capital has a competitive vendor market with modest pricing. The university market (University of South Carolina) drives off-season availability in summer and breaks.
- **Myrtle Beach area (non-resort)** — Resort hotel venues charge hotel rates, but independent event spaces throughout Horry County run considerably less.
- **Spartanburg, Anderson, Rock Hill** — Smaller markets with lower price floors and less competition for dates.

## How to save money on a South Carolina wedding

**Move the date to winter in Charleston.** Charleston winter (December through February, excluding holiday weekends) is warm enough for outdoor events by national standards — highs in the 55–65°F range. Vendor availability improves dramatically and some venues drop rates 20–30% from peak season. If you want a Charleston wedding at a lower cost point, this is the most direct path.

**Choose the Upstate instead of the Lowcountry.** Greenville offers comparable venue quality — restored mills, rooftop terraces, farm properties — at prices 30–40% below Charleston equivalents. You don't get the plantation aesthetic, but Greenville's wedding market has grown considerably and the value is real.

**Research beach permit requirements before booking coastal venues.** Surprise permit requirements discovered after venue selection can add cost and logistical hassle. Confirm permit requirements, guest count limits, and cleanup rules with your county before committing to a public beach ceremony.

**Time outdoor elements around heat.** For summer weddings anywhere in South Carolina, structure the timeline to move guests inside during the hottest part of the day. A ceremony at 6pm followed by an indoor reception is more comfortable for guests and protects florals from heat damage.

**Consider a low-country food station over plated service.** Low-country cuisine lends itself well to interactive food stations — oyster bars, shrimp boils, BBQ setups. These typically cost less per person than full plated service with wait staff, and they match the regional style well.
