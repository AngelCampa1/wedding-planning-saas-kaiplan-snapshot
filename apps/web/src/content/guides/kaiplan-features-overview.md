## What Kaiplan Is Built For

We built Kaiplan because wedding planning tools tend to fall into two categories: lightweight checklist apps that don't go deep enough on the financial and logistical complexity of an actual wedding, and ad-supported platforms that mix planning features with vendor marketplace features in ways that serve the platform's revenue model more than the couple's planning needs.

Kaiplan is a focused tool. It keeps the operational planning work in one place:

1. Budget tracking against actual vendor contracts and payments
2. Vendor management with contact info, payment schedules, and contract notes
3. Guest list with RSVP tracking, dietary notes, households, and plus-ones
4. Seating connected to your RSVP data
5. Public wedding website publishing with RSVP collection
6. Planning checklist tied to your wedding timeline

This article describes each feature in detail, including what it does and what it doesn't do. If you're evaluating whether Kaiplan fits your planning needs, this is the right starting point.

## Feature 1: Budget Tracker

### What it does

The budget tracker is designed to track your actual financial position against your total budget, not just show you estimate percentages.

**Total budget entry:** You set a single total budget number. Everything else is tracked against that number. You can update the total at any time.

**Category budgets:** You can break your total budget into categories (venue, catering, photography, florals, music, attire, hair and makeup, invitations, transportation, honeymoon, miscellaneous, etc.) and set target amounts for each. This gives you a category-level view of whether you're on track or over.

**Vendor line items:** Each vendor you add to the system can be linked to a budget category with an actual quoted or contracted amount. The budget tracker pulls from these vendor records to show you:

- Total contracted spend across all vendors
- Total remaining budget (total budget minus total contracted)
- Category-level totals vs. category budgets

**Deposit and payment tracking:** For each vendor, you can record:

- Total contracted amount
- Deposit amount
- Deposit due date
- Whether the deposit has been paid
- Balance remaining
- Balance due date

The tracker shows you both your total committed spend (contracted amounts) and your total paid-to-date (deposits and payments actually sent). The difference matters: committed spend tells you where your budget stands, paid-to-date tells you your current cash outflow.

**Payment due date visibility:** The dashboard and vendor view surface upcoming payment due dates so you don't miss a deposit deadline or final payment. This is one of the most practically useful features — vendor payment schedules don't align neatly with your memory, and missing a payment due date can put a booking at risk.

**Overage flagging:** When your total contracted vendor amounts exceed your total budget, the tracker flags the overage. You'll see this in the dashboard summary and in the budget section.

### What it doesn't do

- The budget tracker does not connect to your bank account or payment processor. It's a manual ledger — you enter vendor amounts and mark payments as paid yourself.
- It does not generate invoices or send payment reminders to vendors on your behalf.
- It does not handle multiple currencies (wedding in another country with vendors in a different currency requires manual conversion).
- It does not track non-vendor expenses like tips, day-of-spending, or honeymoon costs unless you add them manually as budget line items.

## Feature 2: Vendor Management

### What it does

The vendor management feature gives you a centralized record for every vendor involved in your wedding — from your venue and caterer to your hair stylist and transportation company.

**Vendor profile fields:**

For each vendor, you can record:

- Business name
- Vendor category
- Primary contact name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Website
- Booking status (considering, contacted, quote received, booked, completed)
- Quoted or contracted price
- Contract notes (freeform text for anything relevant from your conversations or contract)
- Deposit amount and due date
- Deposit paid status
- Balance amount and due date
- Confirmation number (for vendors who provide booking confirmation numbers)

**Vendor list view:** The vendor list shows you all vendors across all categories with their booking status, contracted amounts, and payment status at a glance. You can filter by category or status.

**Vendor comparison:** If you're evaluating multiple vendors in the same category (comparing two photographers, for example), you can add both as "considering" or "quote received" with their quotes and notes, then compare them side by side before making a decision.

**Integration with budget tracker:** When you enter a price for a vendor, that amount flows into the budget tracker automatically. You don't need to enter it in two places.

### What it doesn't do

- Kaiplan is not a vendor marketplace. It does not recommend vendors, show sponsored vendors, or earn revenue from vendor referrals. You find your own vendors and add their information to the system.
- It does not store contract PDFs or attachments. Contract notes are freeform text — you'll want to store the actual contract documents in Google Drive, Dropbox, or your email.
- It does not send reminders to vendors or initiate any communication. All outreach to vendors happens outside Kaiplan.
- It does not have vendor reviews or ratings.

## Feature 3: Guest List and RSVP Tracking

### What it does

The guest list feature manages your full list of invited guests from initial roster through final RSVP counts.

**Guest record fields:**

For each guest, you can record:

- First and last name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Household or party association
- RSVP status: pending, invited, accepted, declined
- Linked plus-one guest records
- Dietary restrictions or notes
- Seating assignment (if used)

**CSV import:** If you already have a guest list in a spreadsheet, you can import it via CSV rather than entering every guest manually. The import expects Kaiplan's supported field names, including `first_name`, `last_name`, `email`, `phone`, `side`, `group_name`, `dietary_tags`, and `dietary_notes`.

**RSVP status tracking:** As invitations go out and responses come in, you update each guest's RSVP status. The guest list dashboard shows you:

- Total invited count
- Total accepted
- Total declined
- Total not yet responded

This running count is useful for giving your venue and caterer accurate headcount estimates as your response deadline approaches.

**Dietary notes:** If your caterer needs dietary details, you can store dietary tags and notes on guest records and include them in exports.

**Plus-one management:** You can add a plus-one as a linked guest record, then track that person's RSVP status and dietary notes separately from the primary guest.

**Export:** The guest list can be exported to CSV, which is useful for sharing with your venue coordinator, day-of planner, or for building a seating chart in a separate tool.

### What it doesn't do

- Kaiplan does not send physical invitations or RSVP cards. Physical invitations are handled through a print vendor.
- It does not collect structured meal menu choices. Use dietary tags and notes for caterer context.
- It does not manage registry purchases or registry platform data. The public wedding website can include an external registry URL and note.
- It does not send guest communication (save-the-dates, reminders, thank-you notes) on your behalf.

## Feature 4: Planning Checklist and Timeline

### What it does

The planning checklist gives you a structured set of tasks organized by timeframe before your wedding, with the ability to customize and track completion.

**Default milestone structure:**

Kaiplan provides a default checklist built around standard wedding planning milestones, organized into timeframes:

- 12+ months before the wedding
- 9-12 months before
- 6-9 months before
- 3-6 months before
- 1-3 months before
- Final weeks and day-of

The default tasks cover the major items most couples need to address: booking the venue and key vendors, sending save-the-dates and invitations, finalizing menus, scheduling fittings and trials, confirming logistics, and preparing day-of documents.

**Task customization:** You can add custom tasks specific to your wedding, adjust target dates, mark tasks as complete, and assign tasks to yourself or your partner.

**Timeline view:** The checklist connects to your wedding date, so tasks display in terms of how many days or weeks until your target completion date. Upcoming tasks surface on the dashboard.

**Milestone tracking:** As you complete tasks, your planning progress tracks toward completion. The dashboard shows you a summary of how many tasks are done vs. remaining.

### What it doesn't do

- The checklist does not auto-complete tasks based on other activity in the tool. If you book a vendor in the vendor section, you'll still need to mark the "book venue" or "book photographer" checklist task as done manually.
- It does not have a shared calendar view or integration with Google Calendar or Apple Calendar (you can manually add key dates to your personal calendar from the information in Kaiplan).
- It does not manage day-of timelines or minute-by-minute wedding day schedules. Those are typically handled by your venue coordinator or day-of planner.

## What Kaiplan Does Not Include

For clarity, here is a list of common wedding planning features that Kaiplan deliberately does not include:

**Vendor directory or marketplace:** No sponsored vendor listings, no paid vendor placements, no vendor recommendations. You manage your own vendor relationships.

**Registry integration:** Kaiplan does not manage or link to gift registries.

**Print or digital invitation tools:** No invitation design, print ordering, or digital invite sending.

**Day-of timeline:** No minute-by-minute schedule builder for the wedding day itself.

**Native mobile app:** Kaiplan is a PWA — accessible from any browser, but no native iOS or Android app.

## Who Kaiplan Is For

Kaiplan works best for couples who:

- Want a focused planning tool without vendor advertising influencing their planning decisions
- Have a defined budget they're actively tracking against
- Are managing 6 or more vendors with separate contracts and payment schedules
- Want guest list and RSVP tracking in the same tool as their budget and vendor data
- Are comfortable with a tool that requires manual data entry rather than automated integrations

If your primary need is vendor discovery (finding photographers, florists, or venues you don't already have leads for), Kaiplan is not the right tool — it's a management tool, not a discovery tool. The Knot and WeddingWire are better fits for vendor discovery, though they come with the tradeoffs of ad-supported models.
