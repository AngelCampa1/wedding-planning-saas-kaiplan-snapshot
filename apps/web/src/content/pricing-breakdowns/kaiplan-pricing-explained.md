## What Kaiplan Charges and Why

Kaiplan has no hidden costs. No vendor advertising. No registry commission. No stationery markup. Three tiers: $20/month Starter, $35/month Pro, $100 one-time Lifetime.

The no-hidden-costs claim is worth explaining, because the wedding planning market has conditioned couples to expect that free means something is monetized behind the scenes. The Knot earns from vendor advertising. Zola earns from registry purchases. WeddingWire earns from vendor lead fees.

Kaiplan earns from couples paying for the product. That's it. Which means the only thing the product needs to do is be worth $20/month (or $100 one-time). There are no vendor recommendations to optimize for advertiser revenue. There are no registry commissions influencing which features get built.

## What Starter Includes

Starter at $20/month includes the core planning workflow: budget ledger with actual payment tracking (not just category estimates), vendor management with contract records and payment schedules, guest list with RSVP management and dietary notes, seating chart integrated with the guest list, and a wedding website.

The distinction between Kaiplan's budget tracker and the budget tools in free platforms is worth being specific about. Most free planning apps show you a budget template: "Photography: $3,000 allocated." Kaiplan's budget ledger tracks actual payments: "$900 deposit paid March 12, $2,100 balance due September 1." That's the difference between knowing what you planned to spend and knowing what you've committed to spend and when.

## What Pro Adds

Pro at $35/month adds advanced vendor tracking features, priority support, and early access to new features. For most self-planning couples, Starter covers the full planning workflow without needing the Pro additions.

Pro is for couples managing particularly complex vendor arrangements — multiple venues, many vendors with layered contract structures — or couples who want to be first users of new feature releases.

## The Lifetime Math

Lifetime at $100 one-time is the most economical choice for couples who plan to use Kaiplan through the full planning period.

Break-even against Starter: 5 months ($100 / $20).
Typical wedding planning arc: 12-18 months.
Total Starter cost for 18 months: $360.
Total Lifetime cost for 18 months: $100.

The Lifetime plan isn't sold as a limited-time discount — it's priced to be the obvious choice for couples who know they're going to plan their wedding and don't want to think about a monthly subscription while doing it.

## Comparing Against Free Alternatives

The honest comparison between Kaiplan and free platforms requires accounting for what "free" actually means.

Zola's free tools are real and functional. The trade-off is that Zola's business is registry commerce, and the planning tools are retention features, not the primary product. The budget tracker doesn't track real payments because deep budget management isn't what keeps couples using Zola's registry.

The Knot's free tools are real. The trade-off is vendor advertising — the platform earns from vendors paying for placement, and that relationship influences search results, recommendations, and which vendor categories get prominent placement.

Kaiplan's trade-off is that it costs money. The value proposition: a planning tool built around the couple's workflow, not around vendor advertising revenue or registry commerce. Whether that's worth $20/month or $100 lifetime is a judgment call that depends on how seriously you want to manage the planning process.
