## What You Actually Get for Free

Wedsites' free tier is genuinely functional for a specific use case: building a wedding website on a Wedsites subdomain (yourname.wedsites.com) and collecting RSVPs. The templates are clean. The guest list tool works. For couples who don't care about a custom domain and need basic RSVP functionality, free is real.

The planning tools included in the free tier are thin. A checklist covers the basics of "remember to book these vendors." Budget tracking is category-level — you can note allocations but can't log actual vendor payments, track deposits against a running balance, or manage payment schedules. For couples doing serious budget management, the Wedsites budget tool is a starting template, not a ledger.

## The Hidden Costs

### Domain Registration

The personal and premium plans let you connect a custom domain. The domain itself is purchased separately through a domain registrar — typically $10-20/year for a .com. This cost isn't included in the Wedsites plan price, so the real annual cost of a custom domain on Wedsites is the plan fee plus the domain registration fee.

### Print Invitation Markup

Wedsites facilitates invitation and stationery printing through third-party integrations. Print orders carry a platform margin above direct print costs. For couples who want to compare per-unit print pricing, getting quotes directly from a print vendor alongside Wedsites' facilitated pricing shows the difference.

### Premium Template Unlock

The full template library requires the premium tier. If you see a design on Wedsites you want and it requires the premium plan, the $30/year cost difference between personal and premium becomes relevant.

### The Planning Tool Gap

The most significant "hidden cost" isn't a fee — it's the cost of adding a separate planning tool. Couples who use Wedsites for a wedding website and then need real budget tracking, vendor management, and a seating chart end up paying for two tools. Wedsites at $79/year plus Kaiplan Starter at $200/year is $279/year in total. Kaiplan alone at $20/month covers the wedding website feature plus full planning depth.

## Who Should Use Wedsites

Wedsites works well for couples who primarily need a wedding website — not a planning tool. If you're comfortable doing your real planning in a spreadsheet and just want a clean, shareable wedding website for guests, Wedsites' free or personal tier covers that use case at low cost.

Couples who need both a wedding website and serious planning tools should evaluate whether the total cost of Wedsites plus a planning tool makes sense versus a single tool that covers both.
