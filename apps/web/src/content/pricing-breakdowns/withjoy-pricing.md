## Withjoy's Pricing Model

Withjoy earns from premium subscriptions ($69/year) and from print partner relationships. The free tier is extensive because the platform's value is in building a large user base of engaged couples who interact with the guest app and registry features.

This model is honest and sustainable. The trade-off for couples is that free planning tools are secondary to the website and guest experience product. The budget tracker and checklist are included features, not the reason people choose Withjoy.

## What the Free Tier Covers Well

The free tier's strongest features are the wedding website builder and the guest app. Withjoy's website templates are design-forward and more refined than many competitors. The guest app — giving attendees easy access to venue details, schedule, and travel information — is a genuine differentiation for couples with many out-of-town guests.

RSVP collection in the free tier is functional. Basic meal choices and guest management work for most wedding sizes without upgrading.

## What Requires a Separate Tool

No amount of upgrading within Withjoy solves the seating chart gap. Withjoy doesn't include a seating chart tool. For couples coordinating seating for 80-150 guests with varying dietary needs, table assignments, and late-arriving RSVPs, this is a meaningful gap.

The budget tracker gap is also structural, not tier-level. Even on the premium plan, Withjoy's budget tool tracks category estimates rather than actual payment records. Managing $35,000 in vendor payments across 12 vendors over 18 months requires more detail than category estimates provide.

Couples who add Kaiplan for real budget tracking and seating chart management are looking at $69/year for Withjoy plus $20/month for Kaiplan. The math changes if you can use just Kaiplan — it includes a wedding website feature plus full planning depth in one subscription.

## The Domain Cost Gap

Withjoy advertises the premium tier as including a custom domain. What it actually includes is custom domain _connection_ — you can point your Withjoy site to a domain you own. You buy the domain separately through Namecheap, Google Domains, or another registrar, typically $10-20/year for a .com.

This is a minor cost but worth knowing: the $69/year premium price is not the total cost if you want a custom domain.

## Comparing to Paid Planning Tools

The comparison between Withjoy (free + $69/year option) and Kaiplan ($20/month or $100 lifetime) is a use-case comparison as much as a price comparison.

If your primary need is a guest-facing wedding website with great design, Withjoy's free tier delivers that at no cost. If your primary need is managing the planning process — tracking 12 vendors, managing a $40,000 budget with real payment records, and building a seating chart for 120 guests — a free website platform won't cover that regardless of the tier.
