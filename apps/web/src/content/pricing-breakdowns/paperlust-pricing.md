## How Paperlust's Per-Unit Pricing Works

Paperlust doesn't charge a subscription. You pay per card, per order. The unit price depends on print type, paper stock, finish, and quantity — larger orders get quantity discounts.

For budget planning, the relevant calculation is your total guest count times the pieces you need per guest. A typical invitation package is: one invitation, one outer envelope, one RSVP card, one RSVP envelope. That's four pieces per guest, each priced separately. Some couples add inner envelopes, wedding detail cards, or map inserts — more pieces, more cost.

A 100-guest wedding at standard digital print might look like: 100 invitations at $2.50 each ($250), 100 outer envelopes at $0.50 each ($50), 100 RSVP cards at $1.50 each ($150), 100 RSVP envelopes at $0.50 each ($50). That's $500 before shipping, envelope addressing, or any save-the-date order.

## The Premium Finish Math

Foil and letterpress are the finishes that make Paperlust distinctive. They're also where the per-unit cost climbs quickly.

A foil invitation card at $6-8 each, ordered for 100 guests plus 10% overrun, is 110 cards at $7 = $770 for invitations alone. Add envelopes, RSVP cards, and shipping, and a premium foil suite for 100 guests can reach $1,000-1,400 or more. That's a significant line item and worth budgeting carefully before falling in love with a design that requires premium finishing.

## International Shipping Costs and Lead Time

Paperlust is Australian. Orders to the US ship internationally. That means:

Shipping cost. International shipping on a stationery order runs $25-50+ depending on weight and urgency. For large orders, it can be more.

Production and transit time. Standard production is 5-7 business days, followed by 7-14 business days of international shipping. That's 2-3 weeks minimum from order to delivery, plus your proofing time before production starts. Total calendar window: 4-5 weeks.

That timeline is manageable if planned correctly. It becomes a problem if you start the ordering process late. Save-the-dates should be ordered 4-5 months before the wedding; invitations should be in guests' hands 8 weeks before.

## Stationery as a Budget Line Item

Most wedding budget templates list stationery as 2-3% of the total budget. For a $35,000 wedding, that's $700-$1,050. A full Paperlust suite with save-the-dates, invitations, and envelopes for 100 guests can hit that range or exceed it with premium finishes.

Build the full stationery estimate into your planning budget before ordering — include printing, shipping, and envelope addressing.
