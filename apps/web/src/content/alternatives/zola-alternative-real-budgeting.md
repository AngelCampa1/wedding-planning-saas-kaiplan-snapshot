## What Zola Is Actually For

Zola launched as a registry company. The wedding website, planning checklist, and vendor marketplace came later - added to bring couples onto the platform earlier in the planning process so they would use Zola's registry.

This is not a criticism. Zola's registry is genuinely excellent. Adding items from any retailer, managing cash funds, and tracking gifts is cleaner in Zola than in any competitor. If registry is what you need, Zola is the right tool.

The problem is when couples look to Zola for budget tracking. There is nothing there. Zola does not have a budget tool. No estimate calculator, no ledger, no way to track what you have paid or what you still owe. Every couple using Zola for planning ends up keeping their spreadsheet anyway.

## The Spreadsheet Builder's Experience With Zola

If you are already organizing your wedding in a spreadsheet, Zola's planning tools will feel like a downgrade in most areas. The planning checklist is pre-built and hard to customize. There is no way to add your own budget categories or track vendor payments in a structured way.

The RSVP tools are an upgrade over a spreadsheet. Online form, automatic tallying, guest list integration - this part of Zola is genuinely better than tracking responses in a spreadsheet. The wedding website templates are also better than anything you could build yourself quickly.

But budget and vendor management? Zola does not compete with a spreadsheet, because it does not try.

## What Kaiplan Handles Instead

We built Kaiplan for the budget and vendor management side that Zola leaves to spreadsheets.

**Budget ledger**: Each vendor gets a line with the quoted price, deposit paid, payment schedule, and remaining balance. The running total against your budget stays current as you log payments. This is what your spreadsheet does - Kaiplan does the same thing without formula management.

**Vendor management**: Quotes, contract information, contact details, and payment history in one place per vendor. When you are juggling a florist, caterer, photographer, venue, DJ, and officiant across 12-18 months, consolidated vendor records save real time.

**Seating chart**: Drag-and-drop seating with table layouts. Better than the manual assignment grid that a spreadsheet produces.

You do not need to stop using Zola. Keep Zola for the registry and wedding website. Use Kaiplan for the planning work that requires real numbers.
