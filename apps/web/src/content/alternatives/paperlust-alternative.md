## Understanding What Paperlust Actually Is

This comparison requires a clarification upfront: Paperlust is a stationery company, not a wedding planning app. Couples searching for "Paperlust alternative" are usually in one of two situations: they want a different stationery option, or they've seen Paperlust mentioned alongside planning apps and want to understand where it fits.

If you want a different stationery option, that's a design and print quality conversation, not a planning app comparison. If you want a full planning tool, you need something Paperlust has never claimed to provide.

Paperlust sells premium printed wedding stationery — invitations, save-the-dates, menus, escort cards, table numbers, seating chart prints. The designs are distinctive and the print quality is high. For couples who care about physical paper goods, Paperlust is worth considering seriously.

## What Paperlust Does Well

The print quality argument for Paperlust is real. The platform offers finishes — foil, letterpress, laser cut — that aren't available through most US-based mass-market stationery services. For couples who have a specific aesthetic that requires premium printing, Paperlust's catalog and craftsmanship are competitive with what you'd get from a local print shop.

The digital invitation option is also well done. For eco-conscious couples or those with many guests they can't reach by mail, digital invitations are a practical and increasingly common alternative. Paperlust's digital designs match the quality of their print offerings.

## The Planning Tool Gap

Paperlust does not offer any of the following: budget tracking, vendor management, guest list tools, RSVP collection, seating charts, planning checklists, or any planning functionality.

This isn't a weakness — it's scope. Paperlust chose to build one thing well rather than many things adequately. The trade-off is that couples need a separate tool for everything else.

For budget tracking, vendor management, and guest list management, a planning app is required. Kaiplan handles the full planning workflow: budget ledger with actual payment tracking, vendor management with contract records, guest list with RSVP tracking, and seating chart. At $20/month or $100 lifetime, it's designed to be the planning layer that Paperlust doesn't provide.

## Using Both Together

The natural setup for couples who want premium stationery and a real planning tool is to run them in parallel. Paperlust manages the paper goods. Kaiplan manages the planning workflow.

Here's how the integration works in practice: build your complete guest list in Kaiplan. Once finalized, export or note the count for your Paperlust order quantity. Add your Paperlust stationery order as a line item in Kaiplan's budget tracker, logging the deposit and any balance payments. The vendor entry in Kaiplan tracks your proofing timeline and print delivery date.

This split-tool approach is how couples navigate the reality that no single platform does both premium stationery and deep planning tools well. ## The Stationery Cost Line Item

Stationery is one of the more underestimated wedding budget categories. A 100-guest wedding needs invitations, envelopes, response cards (if including physical RSVP), and save-the-dates. With premium printing and international shipping from Paperlust, a full suite can reach $800-1,200. This is a significant line item that should be built into the budget before invitation ordering begins.
