## The Difference Between Event Management and Wedding Planning

Event management software handles the things all events share: schedules, guest coordination, communication, and logistics. Weddings need all of that. But they also need things specific to the 12-18 month wedding planning arc that general event tools don't account for.

Vendor deposit tracking. Payment schedules across multiple vendors, often with different due dates spread across many months. Seating charts connected to a live guest list that's still being updated as RSVPs arrive. Budget categories that align with wedding-specific spending patterns — venue, catering, photography, florals, music, attire. RSVP management with meal choices and dietary restrictions that later feed into seating decisions.

A general event management tool can hold some of this information. But it wasn't designed for it, and that shows in the features that don't exist and the workarounds you end up building.

## What Wandr Offers

Wandr is a flexible event management platform that handles general event coordination. Its collaboration tools work well for events that involve multiple people coordinating — a wedding planning team, a corporate event committee, or a group of vendors.

For events that aren't weddings, Wandr's general event management approach is an asset. If you're planning a series of events and want one tool to cover all of them, the flexibility matters.

For a wedding specifically, the generality creates gaps. Seating charts, vendor contract management, and multi-month payment tracking aren't generic event features — they're wedding-specific, and tools that aren't built for weddings typically don't include them.

## Variable Pricing Over 18 Months

Wedding planning is a long process — typically 12-18 months from engagement to wedding day. During that period, you need consistent access to your planning tool. The pricing model matters over that horizon.

Variable per-event pricing introduces uncertainty. You don't always know what your cost will be as you add features or manage more complexity. Flat-rate monthly pricing ($20/month at Kaiplan) or lifetime access ($100 one-time) makes the 18-month cost predictable. That predictability is worth something when you're already managing a complex and expensive life event.

## What Wedding-Specific Tools Include That General Tools Don't

Wedding planning apps built for the wedding use case include:

**Seating charts.** Not a generic table assignment tool — a seating chart that knows your guest list, knows who has RSVP'd yes, knows who has dietary restrictions, and lets you drag people between tables as RSVPs change.

**Vendor payment tracking.** Not a contact list — a vendor management system where you can record contract amounts, deposit payments with dates, and upcoming balance-due dates. So you know at any moment what you've committed to pay versus what you've actually paid.

**Budget-to-vendor connection.** Budget lines linked to specific vendors, so when you update a quoted price, your budget updates. When you record a payment, your remaining balance adjusts.

**Guest RSVP to seating connection.** Guest list and seating chart share the same data, so confirming an RSVP makes that guest available in the seating chart without manual re-entry.

These aren't features Wandr or other general event tools typically include, because they're specific to the wedding use case.
