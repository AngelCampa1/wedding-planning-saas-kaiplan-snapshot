# Kaiplan Offering and Pricing

Kaiplan is wedding planning software for couples who want one planning system
without vendor ads. The product centers on a real budget ledger connected to
guests, vendors, seating, and wedding website workflows.

## Current Plans

| Plan | Price | Best fit | Core value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $20/mo or $200/yr | Couples who need budget, guest, and seating basics | Budget ledger, guest list, and seating chart |
| Pro | $35/mo or $350/yr | Couples who want the full planning workspace | Starter plus vendor tracker, wedding website, RSVP, and 2 planners |
| Lifetime | $100 once | Couples planning for more than a few months | Pro access for the wedding without monthly renewal |

## Offer Policy

Kaiplan uses standard pricing. There is no active discount code or automatic
checkout discount.

Do not reference retired discount names, third-party offer fields, hidden
checkout discounts, or code-based onboarding in public copy, docs, email, tests,
or environment setup. If a future promotion is approved, document the active
dates, owner, legal copy, Stripe configuration, and removal plan before
publishing it.

## Positioning

Kaiplan is paid by couples, not vendors. The core message should stay simple:

- One workspace for budget, guests, vendors, seating, website, and RSVP.
- No vendor marketplace and no sponsored vendor placement.
- Clear standard prices that fit the finite shape of wedding planning.
- Lifetime is a straightforward $100 one-time option, not a countdown sale.

## Pricing Math

Use these calculations when writing comparison content:

- 12 months of Starter: $240 monthly or $200 annual.
- 14 months of Starter: $280 monthly.
- 18 months of Starter: $360 monthly.
- 12 months of Pro: $420 monthly or $350 annual.
- 14 months of Pro: $490 monthly.
- 18 months of Pro: $630 monthly.
- Lifetime break-even: 5 months vs Starter, about 3 months vs Pro.

## Funnel Guidance

The pricing funnel should make the next step clear: start a trial, choose a
paid plan, or contact support. Avoid waitlist or cohort language unless the
product deliberately returns to a waitlist-only state.

Near-term pricing work should focus on proof and clarity:

- Show real product workflows for budget, vendors, seating, website, and RSVP.
- Explain export and data retention near the plan CTA.
- Keep Lifetime terms clear and plain.
- Capture pricing objections without changing the public price.

Do not run price tests or seasonal discounts until current standard pricing has
enough conversion data to compare against.
